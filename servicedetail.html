General Check-up

A full check-up with a qualified vet. We look at weight, heart, teeth, skin, ears and eyes, then talk through anything we find.

Duration: 30 minutes

Price: From KES 1,500

Book This Service

What it involves

  • Weight and temperature check
  • Heart and lung listen
  • Teeth, skin and coat check
  • Advice on food and exercise
  • Update to your pet's health record

Benefits

  • Catch problems early
  • Cheaper than treating late
  • Records saved online
  • Peace of mind

How to prepare

  • Bring past vaccine cards if you have them
  • Write down any symptoms
  • Keep dogs on a leash, cats in a carrier
  • Don't feed a big meal an hour before
